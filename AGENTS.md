# Grow an Empire: notes for coding agents

## City polish 0.14.0

**Density 0.14.1:** show one civilian per completed building, using service routes only, regardless of population. The focused harvesting woodcutter replaces that site's civilian to prevent duplicates; construction has its one crew at the unfinished site. Trained army units are exempt: retain recruitment, the existing idle garrison and final combat. Do not reintroduce population-based or extra supply/trade crowds. `syncVillagers` takes completed building IDs, not a population number.

`render/cityTraffic.ts` owns visual citizen trips only: fixed 30 Hz updates, direction-relative lanes, same-edge following, occupied-stop waits and FIFO junction reservations. It never changes simulation resources or recruitment. Preserve trips when buildings add routes; reset on campaign load/restart. Immutable polyline arrays use `render/routeSampler.ts` (cached lengths/binary search) for civilians, garrison and construction crew. `cityLayout.sharedRoadPlan` deduplicates the existing authored branches; draw each edge once and keep supply trips on that plan. This is not an obstacle-checked road planner. Builder/farmer/woodcutter now use walk64 sheets; other roles keep walk32. The art exporter retains old masters but excludes replaced walk32 exports, registers walk64 cells and removes stray boundary fragments. Run `Tools/qa/character_atlases.py` and `Tools/qa/city_motion.py` after changing motion/art. Full-city camera fit, simulation construction timing and saves remain unchanged. See `Docs/SANCTUARY_POLISH.md`.

A short, single-player city builder in Three.js + TypeScript, built for the GoLive web portal (iframe hosting), dressed as a mythic Bharatvarsha epic (v0.6.0). The player makes a campaign's build choices (12–25 on the road; supported campaign limits are 8–25, so read `campaign.moveLimit`, never assume 12), paying for each from the stockpile; the city runs its economy and trains soldiers by itself, then fights one of 25 enemy armies in the muster popup. Wins earn 1–3 stars, and stars unlock the next campaign. The player climbs a realm of 1,008 AI rajas (`realm.ts`) toward rank 1. `CLAUDE.md` points here.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `pnpm start` / `start-game.cmd` | Dev server at http://127.0.0.1:4173 (opens the browser) |
| `pnpm check` | `tsc --noEmit`, ESLint and vitest. **Run before you hand work back.** |
| `pnpm test` | vitest only (includes the exhaustive 12-move offer-path proof and exhaustive early balance and seeded longer campaign calibration) |
| `pnpm lint` | ESLint only (`eslint.config.js`) |
| `pnpm package` | Production build → `release/grow-an-empire-<version>.zip`, then runs the portal bundle validator |
| `pnpm art:export` | Re-export `Assets/Runtime/*.webp` from the PNG masters (Python 3 + Pillow) |

Useful URLs on the dev server: `?reset` (discard the save), `?platform=mock` (fake portal SDK, a guest: no cloud save) and `?platform=mock&auth=email` (signed in, with a localStorage "cloud"), `?perf` (FPS and draw-call readout), `?quality=low`, and `/Tools/dev/iframe-test.html` (portal iframe simulator: pause, resume, session end). `?reset`, `?platform=mock` and `?perf` only work in development and on localhost (`platform/debugFlags.ts`); on the portal they do nothing. `window.__gaeAssetHealth` in the console shows whether art, the font and sound loaded.

## Where things live

- `src/main.ts`: the entry point. Boot order, the render loop (`renderer.setAnimationLoop`), autosave, platform wiring and portal messages.
- `src/game/`: pure rules, no DOM or Three.js.
  - `settlementSimulation.ts` owns all game state (`SettlementState`). **It is the only state owner**: rendering and UI read it and react to its events, and never change resources or moves themselves.
  - `economy.ts` holds every number (costs, output, soldier costs, market prices); `content.ts` is the building catalog, with card text generated from `economy.ts`.
  - `battle.ts` is the strength rule, battle rounds and stars; `campaigns.ts` has the 25 campaigns, the five chapters, the difficulty plan (`targetWinShare`: easy, medium ×3, hard in every chapter, each chapter harder), the enemy mixes of swordsmen, archers and horsemen (`ENEMY_MIX`) and the star unlock rule. Campaign `slug`s are part of save ids: rename the display name, never the slug.
  - `realm.ts` is the Rival Realm: 1,008 seeded AI rajas, seasons (one per battle fought, one per day away, at most 7), ranking by the leaderboard score, overtakes, map counts. Rows sit behind `RealmSource` so real players can replace the AI later. `CAMPAIGN_FIELD` must match the rules (`balance.test.ts` checks it; `calibrate.ts` prints it).
  - `balance.ts` plays every reachable game under a declared policy (default: build when affordable; `gatherPolicy: "every-turn"` also explores strategic Gather) (used by `balance.test.ts` and `Tools/balance/calibrate.ts`; not in the bundle). **Change a number in `economy.ts` and `pnpm test` tells you what it did to every campaign**; rerun `npx tsx Tools/balance/calibrate.ts` to recalibrate the enemies (it prints the seeds and the realm's `field` shares).
  - `saveGame.ts` is the local autosave.
  - `leaderboard.ts` is the player's portal leaderboard standing (score = distinct campaigns won; stars and highest campaign are metadata). main.ts saves it with progress and sends it through `platform/scoreSubmission.ts` to the SDK's weekly `strongplayer` board at battle end. `leaderboard_score` analytics are diagnostic (`Docs/LEADERBOARD.md`).
- `src/platform/`: the GoLive SDK behind one `PlatformAdapter` (golive, local or mock), the cloud+local `progressStore`, and session/portal messages. See `Docs/adr/0001` and `0002`.
- `src/render/`: Three.js scene, sprites, city layout, civic center, villagers, construction views. Art is loaded lazily through `assetCatalog.ts` (`assetUrl("x.png")` resolves to the bundled `.webp`).
- `src/ui/`: the DOM HUD, cards, move summary, keyboard shortcuts and tutorial (`hud.ts`); the briefing, muster/market, popup battle and result dialogs (`campaignFlow.ts`); the 25-stop campaign map (`campaignMap.ts`: a tall painting scrolled up from campaign 1 at the bottom to 25 at the top, with wheel, drag, touch, keyboard, a chapter rail and a "back to campaign N" button; the stops are fractions of the painting in `STOPS`); synthesized sound (`sound.ts`); the icon set (`icons.ts`: inline SVG icons, replaced one by one by commissioned painted ones once `icon-<name>-v1.webp` is bundled; `amount()` = icon + number + a hidden word for screen readers) and building effects as icons (`cardEffect.ts`). `render/popupBattle.ts` animates the already-resolved fight in the muster popup, with bounded painted formations, exact survivor counts, Skip, pause and reduced-motion support (the old city battle, `battleField.ts`/`battleHud.ts`, was removed in 0.10.2); `ui/realmBoard.ts` is the Realm board. `render/combatScene.ts` keeps the unit-icon helpers (`iconStyle`). Display names are Indian (Goshala, Ghee House, Soma Press, Bazaar…), ids are not: code and saves keep `swine-farm`, `butchery`, `wine`.
- `Tools/dev/`: bundle packaging, validator, iframe simulator. `Tools/ArtPipeline/`: art export scripts.
- `developer-kit/`: **current portal SDK guidelines**, read-only reference. `common/` contains historical submission, coding, performance and QA references; do not edit it. The v2 kit supersedes conflicting older SDK guidance.
- `Docs/`: the design and portal docs (`PORTAL_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`, `PERFORMANCE_BUDGET.md`, `STORE_LISTING.md`, `QA_RESULTS.md`, `SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md`, `adr/`). `TECHNICAL_IMPLEMENTATION.md` is partly out of date; its header says which parts.

## Rules that are easy to break

- **Safe economy (v0.10.0):** every completed build/Gather move restores at least 4 wood, 2 stone, 2 grain and 1 ration (`MINIMUM_MOVE_STOCKPILE` in `economy.ts`). Camp supplies must be credited in the move ledger after all spending. Food shortages pause new recruitment; existing soldiers and population never leave during economy moves. Keep old desertion fields for save compatibility; combat casualties still apply.

- **Speed/storage/Gather (v0.8.0):** default speed is 4×; `#speed-slider` indexes 1×/2×/4×/8× and stays visible independently of the city chrome. The Granary costs 4 wood + 2 stone and is offered immediately after a Farm or Mango Grove. Gather is available on every build-choice turn, even with affordable cards. It spends one move, runs the existing economy/upkeep and retains the offers. Difficulty calibration keeps its build-first reference policy; strategic search uses `gatherPolicy: "every-turn"`.

- **Current Hippo Arcade contract (0.12.7):** follow `developer-kit/HIPPO_ARCADE_DEVELOPER_REFERENCE.md` and `developer-kit/SDK_REFERENCE.md` (SDK v2; hosted client currently 2.1.0). Load `/sdk/v2/platform-sdk.js` from the serving origin. Initialize once with client telemetry only, then await `getPlayer()`; no API URL, game ID or token belongs in SDK configuration. Production is `https://www.hippoarcade.com/`; Netlify is test, and their saves/uploads are separate. Always load progress successfully before saving; after any failed save reload and reconcile before retrying. Cloud writes remain at least five seconds apart, under 64 KB UTF-8 and six nesting levels. PREVIEW profiles can save to isolated cloud/browser slots, but never call leaderboards; late preview identity requires reload. Guests remain local-only. Live registered players post `strongplayer` scores at battle end, at least 30 seconds apart, under 2 KB metadata; score is distinct campaigns won, with unchanged reposting after Monday 00:00 UTC reset. Keep AI Realm separate. The portal owns access checks and authentication; no in-game paywall or trial/tier assumptions. Send GAME_READY after drawing, once, to the same-origin parent. Keep landscape listing, portal display names, relative game assets and the 200 MB ZIP limit (target under 50 MB). See `Docs/adr/0003-sdk-v2-portal-bridge.md`.

- **Versions:** every build handed over gets a new version in `package.json` (patch +1 for fixes and small features, minor for a feature set), so `pnpm package` writes a new `release/grow-an-empire-<version>.zip`. Never rebuild over an existing ZIP. History (full list in `CHANGELOG.md`): 0.6.0 Bharatvarsha, realm, difficulty · 0.6.2 move report · 0.6.3 fills the portal iframe · 0.6.7 reliability · 0.7.0 SDK 1.5.0 contract · 0.8.0 speed slider, early Granary, Gather · 0.9.0 popup battle, audio polish · 0.9.1 Gather every turn · 0.10.0 camp supplies, no economy desertion · 0.10.1 action guard · 0.10.2 pre-submission review fixes, asset checks, guests local-only, new key art.

- **Saves:** `SAVE_SCHEMA_VERSION` is 7 (`migrateSnapshot` converts v4, v5 and v6). Changing the snapshot shape needs a new version *and* a migration, and must keep `src/game/__tests__/fixtures/save-v4-mid-construction.json` loading. Never edit that fixture. Stars live beside the snapshot in `SavedGame.campaignStars` (merged with the best of local and cloud).
- **Portal:** asset paths must stay relative (`base: "./"`). No `alert`, `confirm` or `prompt`. No top-level navigation. The SDK script tag in `index.html` must stay. The portal binds the launched game; do not pass a game ID or backend URL to SDK v2.
- **Art:** add new runtime art as a PNG under `Assets/Art/…`, then run `pnpm art:export`. A test fails if a referenced file isn't bundled, or if the WebP is stale. The campaign map `southern-road-map-v6.png` is composed from four campaign paintings by `python Tools/ArtPipeline/make_campaign_road_map.py`; if the art changes, re-place the stops in `campaignMap.ts` and run `Tools/qa/campaign_map.py`.
- **Performance:** only the first screen may block boot (`Docs/PERFORMANCE_BUDGET.md`). Call `requestRender()` after changing anything visible while paused, because the loop skips frames when nothing changed.
- **Layout:** two stage layouts, chosen by `public/boot-stage.js`, loaded before rendering in `index.html` (`<html data-layout>`, `--stage-w/-h/-scale`, `window.__gaeStageScale`, `window.__gaeLayout`). **fixed**: when the frame shows 1280×720 at 95% or more, the game is a 1280×720 stage (16:9) scaled as a whole and centred, with bars on the long side; a 1920×1080 frame shows it at exactly 1.5×. **fluid**: in smaller frames (phones and tablets in either orientation, small windows) the stage takes the frame's own size at 1:1, and the end of `styles.css` rearranges the panels with container queries on `#app` (compact phone layouts put the important things first and the details below them: build panel as a right-hand column in phone landscape, at the bottom in portrait, secondary controls under "More"). Lay things out in stage pixels; respond to size with `@container app (…)` queries, not viewport media queries or `vw`/`vh`. Fluid-only rules are prefixed `:root[data-layout="fluid"]`. `sceneSetup.ts` includes the scale in the pixel ratio and, on phones, fits the city into the area the panels leave free (`freeArea`, which mirrors the panel sizes in `styles.css`: change both together). The HUD publishes its measured size in stage pixels as CSS variables (`--hud-bottom`, `--settlement-bottom`, `--build-panel-reach`). Position panels with those, not fixed pixel offsets. Tap targets stay at least 44 px, and text at least 0.78rem (12.5 stage px). `Tools/qa/viewports.py` checks both layouts at 12 sizes.
- **Build output:** `dist/` is `index.html`, `game.js`, `style.css` and `assets/` (art). It only runs over HTTP: opened straight from a folder, browsers block its scripts and styles. Check a build with `pnpm preview`.
- **Errors:** don't swallow failures silently. Log once with context (`console.warn("[Grow an Empire] …")`) and carry on where the game can. Only the game's own uncaught errors pause a run (`platform/errorFilter.ts`): muted cross-origin errors, the ResizeObserver notice and SDK rejections are reported (`runtime_error`, `where` ending `_external`) and play goes on. Every failure path that shows an error screen also sends `GAME_READY` (once; `notifyPortalReady`), so the portal's loader never hides it. A deliberate click on a card, Gather or Fight undoes a portal pause (`resumeFromPortalPause`).
- **Asset health:** `platform/assetHealth.ts` records image loads and failures (texture loader and `<img>`), the display font (Yatra One) and the sound engine; each problem is reported once (`asset_problem`). The bundle validator fails if code or styles name an `assets/…` file the ZIP lacks; `Tools/qa/assets_audio.py` decodes every bundled file and plays a campaign checking requests, images, the font and sound.
- **Icons only after the tutorial:** text and icons while the player is learning (`<html class="words">`), icons only afterwards, on every screen size. Mark words that only help learners `class="w"` (or `ws` to keep them for screen readers), give icon buttons an `aria-label`, show every amount with `amount()`, and put sentences behind the card's "i" / long press or a dialog's Details fold (`.flow-extra`). Each phone screen has a word budget (`Docs/MOBILE_UX_PLAN.md`, checked by `Tools/qa/text_budget.py`).
- **Move report:** from move 2 the build panel first shows the last move's report (`renderReport` in `hud.ts`: one line per building from `MoveSummary.ledger`, then each good before → after, then warnings) and the cards only after Choose (or Enter; keys 1–3 do nothing on the report, so a quick "1, 1" can't build an unseen card). Focus moves to Choose. After a completed tutorial, words stay on until the first report has been read. QA scripts get past it with `flow.past_report`.
- **UX analytics:** `ux_*` events (`Docs/PLAYTEST_PORTAL.md`) are how we learn whether players understand the screens. Keep them when changing the cards or dialogs.
- **City motion (0.10.3):** commuters run on `crowdElapsed` (main.ts; real time × √speed), not the game clock; walk poses come from the distance walked (`strideFrame`, `getWalkFrame`), so a sheet with more poses (walk64/work16, `Docs/ANIMATION_COMMISSION_BRIEF.md`) drops in without code changes. Service-route villagers keep their own lane (`laneOffset`) and stop short of the Town Hall (`hallStop`). Roads widen ×1.05 per civic level up to `ROAD_GROWTH_CAP` (1.6), eased by `cityLayout.update`; lanes follow `roadScale`.
- **Buildings 0.10.4:** Baoli (+1/+2/+3 at each Farm, Mango Grove and Goshala as it ages, `baoliBonus`), Prakara (fells 25% of the enemy before the battle, `watchtowerVolley`), Gajashala (a war elephant on completion and every 6 moves; `state.elephants`, optional so older saves load; fights as 4 swordsmen via `effectiveCounts`, eats 2 rations), Dharma Stambha (+20% player strength; requires every war building, `WAR_BUILDINGS`, and is offered first once eligible). All battle conditions come from `SettlementSimulation.battleSetup()`; `balance.ts` mirrors them in `bestMargin`, so the realm field and calibration stay honest. Their art is a procedural stand-in (`Tools/ArtPipeline/make_standin_buildings.py`); painted art replaces it under the same filenames (`Docs/BUILDING_ART_BRIEF.md`).
- **Buildings 0.10.5:** Burj (militia strength 1 and cap 14 via `BattleModifiers.militiaStrength` / `militiaCap`), Sarai (`planSwap` buy multiplier 1.5, +1 Bazaar sale), Koshagar (interest `floor(gold/5)` ≤ 6 after the market; `sellswordCap` +2), Dharmashala (`dharmashalaPeople`, +4 `militiaCap`, `soldierVillagers` saves every 4th). `resolveBattle`/`playerStrength`/`gapToTarget` take a `BattleModifiers` object, not a number. Offer refill (`nextBuildingOffer`): urgencies → direct follow-ups → `OFFER_FAMILY` rotation (war, land, town by move) → `offerMove` → `offerPriority`; every new building needs a family (content test). `Tools/balance` endings carry `burj` and `koshagar`.
- **Browser QA:** `Tools/qa/*.py` share `Tools/qa/flow.py` (map → briefing → city, play a move, play to the muster, fight). Update it, not each script, when the flow changes.

- **Script policy 0.12.3:** the live portal blocks inline scripts. Keep index.html script tags external only. Ordered classic boot-stage.js / boot-watchdog.js files under public/ run before the SDK and module entry; Vite copies them unchanged and adds version queries. Do not turn them into deferred modules (fit/watchdog must run early). The validator rejects inline blocks; portal_iframe.py and boot_csp.py enforce script-src without unsafe-inline/unsafe-eval in local QA.

- **City spacing 0.12.4:** Dharmashala is behind the Town Hall at planPoint(1000, 270), House at (700, 625), Bazaar at (1200, 650). Keep their positions identity-keyed in cityLayout.ts so restored saves, roads, building sprites and commuter routes agree.

- **Campaign ratings 0.15.0:** defeat earns zero; victory earns one star plus independent bonuses for no population issues and no resource issues across the entire run. `campaignRating.ts` owns the rating; `SettlementState.campaignHealth` records affected moves and first occurrences. Zero recruitment from no free villagers is a population issue; zero production/recruitment from missing inputs or unpaid army upkeep is a resource issue. Low reserves, spending and partial output do not count. Record before camp supplies. Preserve best stars. Migrated unfinished older runs have unknown history and require a fresh replay for clean-run bonuses; completed legacy results remain intact. Legal three-star witnesses for all 25 campaigns are regression-tested. Battle strength margins remain relevant to combat and historical AI Realm bands, not player stars.
