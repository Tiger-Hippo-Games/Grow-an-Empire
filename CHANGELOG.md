# Changelog

Every build handed over gets a new version and its own ZIP in `release/` (never rebuilt over). Newest first. Test evidence for each version is in `Docs/QA_RESULTS.md`.

| Version | Date | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| 0.10.5 | 2026-10-11 | The other four buildings from the proposal, so all eight are in (Mandir replaced by the Dharma Stambha): **Burj** bastion (militia fight at full strength, cap 10 → 14); **Sarai** caravanserai (Bazaar swaps at 1.5× instead of 2×, one more Bazaar sale a move); **Koshagar** treasury (+1 gold a move per 5 stored, at most 6; +2 sellswords); **Dharmashala** rest house (+6 people, +2 a move, +4 militia, every 4th soldier needs no villager). Baoli strengthened (+2/+3/+4, and the Royal Kitchen, Ghee House and Soma Press run one more cycle). New offer rule: after the must-haves (Granary after a Farm, the two trainers, the Ashvashala, the Dharma Stambha) and the direct follow-ups of what you just built, the refill rotates between land, town and war families; the Bazaar opens from any food or soma processor; the Sarai joins the land family. All 24 buildings are offered on at least 5% of build-order paths. All 25 campaigns and the Realm field recalibrated. Stand-in art for the four, generated by the same script. |
| 0.10.4 | 2026-10-11 | Four new buildings (Ravi's choices of 2026-10-11): **Baoli** stepwell (+1 at every Farm, Mango Grove and Goshala, growing to +2 and +3 as it ages); **Prakara** watchtower (fells 25% of the enemy before the battle; shown at the muster, in the battle and the report); **Gajashala** (a war elephant on completion, then one every 6 moves; fights like 4 swordsmen, eats 2 rations); **Dharma Stambha** (+20% army strength in the battle; offered only once the Lohar Forge, Bow Hall, Akhara, Ashvashala and Gajashala all stand). No temple. Offer rules retuned so every building still appears: Soma Press now opens from the Mango Grove, the Royal Kitchen from move 3, the House earlier in its tie-break, the Prakara from a Granary and Quarry. All 25 campaigns and the Realm field recalibrated. Procedural stand-in art for the four buildings and the elephant, with an art brief (`Docs/BUILDING_ART_BRIEF.md`). Saves from 0.10.3 load unchanged (schema 6). |
| 0.10.3 | 2026-10-11 | Smoother, less crowded city: walk poses follow the distance walked (no foot sliding, no flicker at 8×), a gentle step bob, a calmer crowd clock (√speed), each villager in its own lane and stopping at its own distance short of the Town Hall, nearer villagers drawn in front. Roads widen 5% per completed move, compounding, capped at 1.6×, eased in. Drop-in support for smoother sheets (walk64: 8 poses; work16: 16 frames) and an art brief (`Docs/ANIMATION_COMMISSION_BRIEF.md`). `start-game.cmd` no longer re-installs (or fails) on a version bump. |
